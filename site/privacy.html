Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026

Short version: Video Text collects nothing, transmits nothing, and contacts no servers. Optical character recognition runs entirely inside your own browser, offline. There is no account, no analytics, and no third-party service of any kind.

Data we collect

None. The extension has no backend, no analytics SDK, and makes no network requests whatsoever — not at install, not at startup, and not during use. We do not receive your video frames, your extracted text, your browsing history, your IP address, or any identifier.

Data the extension stores on your device

Two things are saved locally, using Chrome's own extension storage. Neither ever leaves your computer.

WhatWhereWhy
Your captures chrome.storage.local The text you extract, plus its video timestamp, page title and URL, so your library persists and exports can include timings and sources.
Your settings chrome.storage.sync Preferences like OCR language and upscale factor. If you have Chrome Sync enabled, Google syncs these across your own signed-in browsers, exactly as it does your bookmarks. We never see them.

Delete everything at any time with Clear all in the library, or by removing the extension — uninstalling deletes its local storage.

Video frames

When you press Grab text, the extension copies the pixels inside your selection into an in-memory canvas, passes them to a recognition engine that is bundled inside the extension itself, and discards the image. The frame is never written to disk and never uploaded. The OCR engine (a WebAssembly build of Tesseract) and its language model ship inside the package, which is why the download is comparatively large — and why it works with no internet connection at all.

Permissions, and why each is needed

PermissionReason
activeTabLets the overlay appear on the tab you're watching, only after you invoke the extension by shortcut, toolbar click or context menu.
scriptingInjects the selection overlay into that tab when you activate it.
storageSaves your captures and settings locally, as described above.
offscreenHosts the OCR worker. Manifest V3 service workers cannot reliably run WebAssembly workers, so recognition happens in an offscreen document.
downloadsWrites the export file (.txt, .srt, .docx…) when you click Export. Files are generated locally and saved directly to your machine.
clipboardWriteCopies extracted text when you press Copy.
contextMenusAdds the “Extract text from this video” right-click item.

The content script is registered for all sites because a video can appear on any website, and the extension cannot know in advance where you'll want to use it. It only renders a selection overlay in the page; it does not read, collect or transmit page content. The extension deliberately declares no broad host_permissions — access is limited to the tab you actively invoke it on.

Third parties

There are none. No advertising, no trackers, no crash reporting, no remote code. Manifest V3 forbids remotely hosted code, and this extension executes only the files inside its own package.

Children

The extension collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes it will be updated here, with a new date at the top. Since the extension is open source, any change in data handling is also visible in the commit history.

Contact

Questions or concerns: open an issue on GitHub. The complete source code is available at github.com/PulseInAir/Video-Text and can be independently audited.
