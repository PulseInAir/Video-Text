Notes

  • Works on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, X, Vimeo, Twitch, Reddit, LinkedIn, Coursera, Udemy and any other page with an HTML5 video.
  • DRM-protected players (Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+) return black frames to every extension — that is a browser-level restriction, not a bug here.
  • Shortcuts can be changed at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
